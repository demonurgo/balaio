Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Draft for review. The parts in brackets still need to be confirmed.

Balaio is a monthly grocery list that can be shared with other people in real time, available at balaio.demonurgo.com and in the iPhone app. This policy explains what data Balaio keeps, what it is used for and how you can delete it. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and, where they apply to you, the data protection laws of your country, such as the European Union's GDPR.

Who looks after your data

Balaio is maintained by an independent developer, [name of the person responsible], who is the data controller. Contact: [contact email].

Data we collect

  • Account: first name, last name, date of birth, email and password. The password is stored only as a hash (Argon2); nobody can read it.
  • Profile photo, if you choose one.
  • Content you create: lists, budgets, items, quantities, prices, categories, notes, item photos and your pantry.
  • Shared list membership: which lists you are part of and who changed each item.
  • Technical data needed for the service to work, such as your IP address in server logs, used for security and to limit sign-in attempts.

What we use it for

Only to make Balaio work: signing you in, storing and syncing your lists, showing changes in real time to everyone on the same list and protecting the service from abuse. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for when you created your account.

Balaio has no ads, does not sell data, does not track you across apps or websites and does not use third-party analytics tools.

Who the data is shared with

  • People on the same list: when you join a shared list through an invite, the other people on it see your name, your profile photo and the list's content. Your email and date of birth are not shown to them.
  • Hosting: the data is kept on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH [confirm server location], which only stores and processes it on our behalf.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

Cookies and storage on your device

The website uses a single session cookie, needed to keep you signed in. The iPhone app keeps the access token in the device's Keychain. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the data is removed from the live database right away. Backups, if any, are overwritten within [period] days.

How to delete your account

In the app or on the website, open Profile → Delete account and confirm with your password. This deletes:

  • your account, photo and pantry;
  • the lists you created, for everyone who was on them;
  • your membership in other people's lists (the items you added to them remain, without your name).

If you can't sign in, ask for deletion through the contact email.

Your rights

You can ask for confirmation that we process your data, and for access, correction, portability, deletion and information about sharing. You can do much of this in the app itself (edit profile and delete account); for the rest, write to the contact email. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Balaio is not directed at children. [Define a minimum age to create an account, if any.]

Security

Communication is always encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored as hashes and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident affects you, we will let you know as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and, for important changes, we will let you know in the app.
