Frequently asked questions

How do I share a list?
Open the list, tap Share and send the invite link. Whoever opens the link and signs in can edit the list with you. The link is valid for 7 days.

How do I leave a shared list?
Open the list, tap Share and choose Leave list. The list's owner can also remove people.

I forgot my password.
There is no automatic recovery yet. Write to the email above from your account's address.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm with your password. The lists you created are deleted for everyone; on other people's lists, you just leave. See the privacy policy.
